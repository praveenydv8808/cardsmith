Cardsmith notes → cards, you decide

Paste your notes, get a first draft of cards.

Drop in a lecture transcript or a page of notes. Cardsmith pulls out the key bits and turns them into flashcards and quiz questions, each one kept next to the sentence it came from. You decide what's good before anything gets saved.

Everything runs here in your browser, so your notes stay on your device. New drafts show up in Review for you to approve, fix, or toss.

Check each card against your notes.

Every draft sits right next to the sentence it came from, so a quick glance tells you if it's right. Keep the good ones, fix the close ones, drop the rest. This is the part that keeps your library honest.

Your approved cards.

All the cards you kept, in one searchable place. Tap one to see its answer and where it came from.

Quiz yourself.

Pulls from the cards you've kept. Read the prompt, try to recall the answer, then say how it went. Cardsmith keeps score for the round.
